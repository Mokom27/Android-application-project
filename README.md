# CarbonTrack - Student Carbon Footprint App

**A simple app to help university students track and reduce their carbon footprint from daily spending and habits.**

## About CarbonTrack

CarbonTrack is designed specifically for university students to make climate action easy and relatable. It helps students see the environmental impact of their everyday choices — such as buying food, taking transport, shopping, and paying for electricity — without requiring complicated manual tracking.

The goal is to turn climate awareness into **practical, affordable action** that fits student life and budget.

## Key Features

- **Dashboard** – View your daily and weekly carbon footprint
- **Campus Ranking** – See how you compare with other students
- **Squads** – Create or join groups with friends for friendly competition
- **Campus Challenges** – Join weekly challenges (e.g., Reduce 5kg CO₂)
- **Eco Garden** – Watch a virtual campus garden grow as you reduce emissions
- **Habit Logging** – Manually log eco-friendly actions (reusable cup, walking, etc.)
- **Student-Friendly Tips** – Practical suggestions like using campus shuttle, meal prepping, or digital textbooks
- **Share Impact** – Share your progress with friends

## How the App Tracks CO₂

The app tracks CO₂ in two ways:

- **Automatic tracking**: It matches your spending transactions (food, transport, shopping, etc.) with **average carbon emission values** from standard databases. For example, it knows roughly how much CO₂ a ride or a meal produces.
- **Manual habit logging**: Users can also log actions that aren’t captured by spending, such as using a reusable cup or walking to class.

> **Note**: The numbers are estimates based on averages. It’s not 100% exact yet, but it gives a clear picture and practical suggestions to help students make better choices.

## Why This Matters

Most carbon tracking apps target adults with higher incomes. CarbonTrack is built for students — focusing on limited budgets, campus life, peer influence, and realistic changes. It makes sustainability **visible, competitive, and fun** instead of stressful.
